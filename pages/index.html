Reading Club: The Good Life

Six weeks. Six traditions. One question: How should we live?

Most of us have books we have always meant to read. The hard part is actually reading them, understanding them, and finding people to think with.

That is what Reading Club is for.

Over six weeks, we will read selections from six great traditions:

  • Aristotle
  • Confucius
  • Laozi
  • Proverbs
  • Bhagavad Gita
  • Dhammapada

Each week includes a carefully chosen reading, a weekly lecture, and two small-group reading sessions. You will read alongside the same community throughout the program, so the conversations can build from week to week.

Throughout the six weeks, we will return to one question:

How should we live?

This is not a lecture series where you simply show up and listen. It is a reading community built around serious study, conversation, and reflection.
